SCOPE AND A WARNING ABOUT WHICH FRAMES ARE CURRENT
I read HARNESS.md (all the sections you named), EVALUATOR_BRIEF.md, and looked at: page2-1 to page2-10, base-1 to base-8, flag-1, choose-1 and choose-3, pop-3 and pop-7, piece-1 and piece-5, hom-slime/wood/water/acid-game, run-3-suppliers, run-1-take-one, eval-pick-screen, suppliers-six, sigil-unlock, kit-1-start, pick-a-molecule, equipment-all-40-now, one-homunculus, 5-batch-lost.

PER-SCREEN FAULTS

A. page2-4 (the bare game round page, 800 px wide)
1. Right column, five reaction lines at x 570-780. The lines are drawn as pills ("C=C -> OH") with the word under each ("hydrate", "oxidize", "hydroborate", "reduce"). Breaks "objects, icons and short tokens, almost no sentences" and "Words where an object or mark should be". A new player cannot read C=C or OH, and the verb in small grey type is a label, not a mark. Fix: draw the change as two tiny molecule pieces with an arrow (the bond turning, the OH appearing), no verb, and show the name only on hover.
2. The same pills are colored discs. The ellipse "pills" (blue, red, orange) are the "discs, circles or pills" class and look like Balatro chips. Fix: draw the group as an inked ball-and-stick fragment, not a filled capsule.
3. Lines 1-4 are grey and faded, line 5 alone is orange and underlined, with the "+C=O" fragment. The grey state reads as "disabled" with no reason a first-time player could name. Fix: separate "not usable on this molecule" from "not chosen" by drawing a small crossed bond on the unusable ones, or removing them.
4. The "1 2 3" numbers to the left of the lines compete with the "1 2 3" round rings at the top left and the "3" token ring. Three different 1-2-3 systems on one page.
5. Molecule is about 300 px wide on an 800 px frame, sitting at a 15-degree slant with the right-hand reaction column crowding in. Atom radius is small and bond length uneven, and the little white H and "O" label on the orange atom are tiny. Fix: give the molecule the whole middle of the page and move the reaction list out of its eye line.
6. The yellow/orange/blue atoms have tiny "*O" or "O" glyphs on them. At this size the glyph is unreadable, and "*O" is a mystery mark. Fix: drop the glyph and rely on colour, or print the element letter only on hover.
7. Bottom flag line: thin line, 1,300 in red, three grey flags, and a coin oval at the end with an empty egg ring then "0". The "0" with the empty oval is a nameless mark. The flag numbers 2,600 / 5,200 / 10,000 sit tight to each other and to the little tan ovals. Fix: bigger flags, with the coin pay-out drawn on the flag pole itself.
8. Left stack: "round 1", circled 1 2 3, three ring tokens, arrow, a faded download icon, the "0". Five small things stacked in 150 px, all different sizes and weights. The faded download-tray icon above the 0 is a nameless mark.
9. Dead space: the whole top third of the page is empty, and the right side below the list is empty. The composition sits low and left.
10. The big black "0" is a heavy italic brush stroke that looks different in weight from the red "1,300" and from "round 1". Three sizes of the same pen with three different slants.

B. page2-1, 2, 3, 10 (the bare game's small mid-play frames, 560 px wide, and a stepped sequence)
11. page2-2: "+142" prints directly across the middle of the molecule, over atoms and bonds, together with "1" above and a black dash under the carbonyl. This is "anything covering the molecule", the biggest sin in the ruling ("Nothing may hide the reaction"). The dashes are unnamed marks, and the diagonal reads as a stretched or broken bond. Fix: either draw the bond breaking as a clear gap with the two ends labelled, or remove.
13. page2-2/3/10: "+20" floats alone at the right with no object attached. A first-time player cannot say what it is the plus of.
14. page2-1: the faded "C=C -> OH" pill with a small curl mark floats at the top right of the molecule, unattached to the list. It is the same pill problem again but now sits over empty space.
15. page2-1: the molecule here (about 220 px) is tiny and the first atom is a pink one with no stated element. The pink atom (maybe P or S) and the orange one change colour between frames without a key. Not a fault if it is the periodic scheme, but nothing teaches it.

(2) TEN THINGS THAT WOULD IMPROVE HOW THE GAME LOOKS BEYOND FAULT-FIXING
1. Make the molecule the hero. One fixed stage, centre-left, 55% of the page width, with a soft paper shadow beneath it (one soft ellipse, not hex tiles). Scale atoms to fit the chain, with bond length constant. Every other element is arranged around that stage.
2. A fixed gain lane. All score numbers rise from the atom that made them, go up in a straight line 60 px, then slide to the score along a ruled line that is outside the molecule's box. Never pile: queue them so each number shows for about 300 ms. This is the Balatro "count up piece by piece" and the Raccoin cascade.
3. Draw the reaction as a before/after pair of small ball-and-stick fragments with an ink arrow, in the same renderer as the molecule. Delete verbs. On hover show a one-word name in the pen face. This makes the right column look like part of the same world and teaches the game without sentences.
4. Make the multiplier an object. Two inked stacks or a bar that stretches as the chain grows, with the number in the heavy brush. Remove all "times" words. The bar grows each time a link in the chain fires, and its colour warms from grey to red as it passes the flags.
5. Show the chain: when a reaction fires, flash a short ink line from the changed atom to the next atom it will set off (a quick pen stroke, then fading), so the cause of each jump is drawn on the molecule, not told. Use the owner's allowed "mark" (a drawn line), not a circle.
6. One lettering system, two weights: a heavy brush for numbers and flag values, a plain thin hand for names. Pick one baseline grid and one ink colour (a warm near-black), and reserve red for the goal and the lost state only, green for passing, blue for nothing but the atom N. Remove navy entirely.
7. Rebuild the menu screens as places on the same paper: the pick-one-of-two as two molecules on the desk with the flags planted next to them like a map; the take-one-of-three as three objects (the actual reagent bottle, the equipment, the upgrade) with a drawn price in coins and no text; the supplier page as a shelf of the actual drawings, each hung with its price in coins. Use the equipment art, which is the best asset.
8. A real end-of-round beat: the molecule slides to centre, the flags stand up in order, each coin earned drops into the purse one by one, and the word (stable, unstable, volatile, overreact) is written in the brush face once, large, with no halo. A loss shows how far short the bar fell, with the bar drawn to scale.
9. Redraw the creatures in the same hand as the equipment (thick ink, flat colour, one highlight, no faces beyond two dots), keep them at the shelf's edge at 90 px and let them hop beside the chain, not on it. Give the jars a visible fill level in place of the "0/150" text.
10. Put the whole page on a consistent notebook sheet: a slightly darker paper tone, a visible ruled margin, flags and the bar drawn as a hand-ruled line with tick marks, used tokens crossed with ink the way a notebook strikes a line (draw the strike as a pen stroke, not an X in a ring). Atoms get a faint contact shadow so they sit on the paper. Add a light page texture so the 3D molecule and the 2D ink share a surface.
