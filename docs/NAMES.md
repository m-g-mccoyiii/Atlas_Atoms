# Overreact: what the pieces of the game are called

Proposed 2026-11-11 after the owner said: "You focus so hard on saying the same things as me. Call the game elements their own thing based thematically on them and design criteria." Until the owner rules otherwise these are the names used in the game, in docs and in talk. The working words in the right column are retired.

Criteria for a name: it comes from a real lab or from alchemy; it says what the thing does in the game; it is short enough to sit on the page as a token; it is not the stock word other games use.

| The piece | Its name | Why | Retired working word |
|---|---|---|---|
| the points a round makes | yield | what a chemist calls how much came out of a reaction | score, total, points |
| the least a round's molecule must meet to go on | the spec | a product either meets spec or it does not; meeting it is the floor, and nobody is told off for exceeding it | hurdle, win condition, target, goal |
| the four yield marks | stable, unstable, volatile, overreact | set by the owner | flags, marks |
| a reaction the player fires | a reagent | the bottle on the shelf; already the game's word | reaction card |
| what the molecule does by itself | a reflex | it happens without being told, at once, every time | self-reaction, cascade kind, zip |
| a run of reflexes setting each other off | an overreaction | the game's own name for its own best moment | chain, cascade, combo |
| something the molecule has that can be pointed at and counted (a sugar, a run of alternating double bonds, a ring, a cage) | a motif | a lab word for a repeating unit; more of it pays more; see `docs/tools_chem/TRAITS.md` | family, trait, kin |
| a lab object or small molecule used on the molecule | an instrument | a thing a person picks up and uses on the work | helper, piece, equipment |
| a creature hatched from a jar | a homunculus | alchemy's little made creature; the owner's word | pet, creature, familiar |
| what comes off the molecule | runoff | what leaves the flask and is collected | by-product |
| money inside a run | gold | what alchemy was always after | coins |
| what stays between runs to spend | credit | a lab's account with its suppliers; already built | |
| a round that changes a rule | an inspection | someone walks in and the usual way of working is not allowed today | special round, assay, boss |
| the three lengths of run | bachelor's (3 rounds), master's (6), doctorate (9) | each is won to open the next, and each asks for more | tier |
| the harder levels after a doctorate | hazard 1 to 8 | the hazard rating on a bottle; higher is more dangerous | stake, ascension |
| a thing done in play that unlocks something for good | a finding | a result that goes in the record and opens new work | deed, achievement |
| a way to start a run | a notebook | each one sets up the bench differently | kit, deck |
| the record of real compounds made | the catalog | what a supplier lists; an entry is filled when the compound is made | the book |
| the screen between runs | the bench | where everything kept is laid out | hub, menu |
| one turn, one molecule's three turns, one whole game | page, round, run | kept: the owner asked for these to stay clear and apart | |
