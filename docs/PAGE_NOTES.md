### How to rig a creature (about 110 lines and half a day each)

1. **Parts** (`RIG.<id>` in `build`, about 35 lines). Keep these names: `dress`, `face` and `ambient` look for them. Copy the numbers from its `PUSH` drawing into `form(stage)`; a part a stage does not name is not there.
2. **Motion** (`CLIP.<id>`, about 45 lines). `rest`: write `S(lag)` (breath, weight shift, the landing ring), call `K.dress`, then the stage's own small habit, `K.face`, `K.ambient`. `act`: its own GAIT in `trip` (acid hops; a slime should ooze, hydrogen drift, salt shuffle) and its own HANDS-ON work at the molecule; give back `e`, `y`, `lunge`. `evo`: one line of `RULE` per stage: which part comes on which beat and how (`m` tween, `p` grow, `d` drop in, `u` rise, `a` eaten, `g` gone).
3. **The page** (`scheme.js`, about 30 lines): an `A20ACT.<id>` that sets `j.r21`, `j.bites`, the standing place in `after`, and draws only what is touched; a line in `R21T`; a `JLIFE.<id>` wrapper. `r21Frame`, the `homJump` and `homEvo19` wrappers serve every rig as they are.
