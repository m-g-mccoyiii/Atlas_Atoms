# Overreact: four independent reviews, 2026-11-11

Each reviewer read `EVALUATOR_BRIEF.md` and `PASS.md` and was not told what had changed.

## The screen (sections 4 and 5)

Section 4 (spectacle and speed): pass on the visuals, but the speed gates were not measured here. Section 5 (the page): fail.

**What a first-time player would understand**
- pop-4 to pop-8: the count at right (2, 3, 4, 5, 6) and the 14,948 / 1,300 / 12.1 rising together read as a chain building. The blue bar across the molecule reads as the link being made. The strip is clippable.
- choose frames: two molecules, a big red number each, and coins on offer. "Pick the bigger" is obvious, though the red numbers do not explain what they are (the score to beat?).
- hom frames: a cartoon creature with a face walks the molecule. The "hydrogen", "water" and "dry ice" droplet labels are cryptic. The score at left is 265 against a beat of 6,500, so the player cannot tell what the creature does.

**Sentences and text on screen**
- "click: +1 use" is a sentence, under the balloon in pop-5 to pop-8. It breaks the rule.
- "mult +0.2", "mult +0.15", "twice", "go", "zip", "beat" and "hydrogen" are word labels. "zip" shows up twice in pop frames: bottom-left and under the big count.
- "twice" and "mult +0.15" are small and cramped under the ring and chain headings.
- Overlaps:
  - "+50" and "+0.1" float over or beside the 14,948 and the multiplier in pop-4 to pop-8. "+50" overlaps the "1,300" area in pop-7 and collides with the score in pop-8.
  - In pop-8 the ghost "+50" sits on the molecule's lower area.
  - In hom-hydrogen the creature's body sits on the molecule's bonds.
  - In hom-co2 the cube creature overlaps the atoms.
  - Atom labels such as "+O" are tiny and unreadable.

**Rejected looks**
- The tokens are not circles drawn on the molecule, and there are no glows or serifs. But there is a yellow highlighter bar under 390 in choose-3, a hard-edged thin blue bar across the molecule, the "go" arrow, and dark navy as the dominant ink color. The reel-style number panel is close to a Balatro scoreboard.
- Atoms with faces are not present. The creatures are separate from the atoms, but they sit on the molecule.

**Does the molecule read?** In choose frames, yes, clearly. In pop frames the 3D molecule is small and thin, with a lot of empty space. In the hom frames it is cluttered by the creature and the ring shapes, and it is barely readable in hom-co2.

**Frame choice**
- I would show pop-6 (or pop-7) to a friend. The count, score and multiplier all move together and the molecule is clear.
- The worst is hom-co2-game. The creature covers atoms, the labels are cryptic, and the page is mostly empty.

**Section verdicts**
- Section 4: pass, narrowly. The chain is visible on the molecule, with a growing count and bar, and the pace looks fast. I could not time "twenty in under four seconds" from still frames.
- Section 5: fail. There is a sentence on screen ("click: +1 use"), many word labels, overlapping floating numbers, cryptic labels and tokens, and heavy navy. The round page is not explainable cold: "beat", the ladder of 340,000 / 170,000 / 86,000 with its pills, and the three colored dots are all unexplained.

**Three things to fix, most important first**
1. Remove "click: +1 use" and replace the word labels (go, zip, mult, twice, beat, and the creature names) with objects or icons. Explain the ladder and the dots without sentences.
2. Fix the overlaps: "+50" and "+0.1" over the score and multiplier, and the creature on top of the molecule.
3. Cut the navy dominance, the yellow highlighter and the hard blue bar. Make the molecule larger and centered, so the hom frames read as clearly as the choose frames.

## The rules (sections 2 and 3)

**Section 2, decisions: pass, narrowly.**

A page is a puzzle in the rules. Five dealt reactions, three slots, and three uses each. A zip reopens when the page turns. Oxidize fired first kills the chain, and fired second it is safe. A step pays in full only past the longest of the round, so pages two and three must lengthen the engine or they pay little. Redraw is the verb for a bad deal.

The doubts are about evidence and legibility:
- The turn measures (second option within 30%, breaking on purpose) were not run on the final rules. The last logged figure is 44.1%, under the gate.
- No number shows before firing, so some players will see guessing, not planning.
- The pay rule is stated three ways: a quarter in CASCADES.md, "55 in 100" in PASS.md, and pays-in-full-past-the-longest. A player could not predict it from the page.

**Section 3, pieces combine: fail.**

Only five of sixty pieces are live, plus three passive ones. The gates were written for sixty and were not measured. The rules do not support five distinct builds. Four are real and the fifth exists only on paper:
1. **One long zip:** burner, burette and magnet make a single chain. It pays on page one when the chain runs the whole length.
2. **Many zips:** mirror and seed crystal. This is the best pair, at 5.90 steps against 3.77 and 4.29. It pays when two starts run on one page.
3. **Dry ice engine:** oxidize on a chain-end C=C feeds carbon dioxide, and the frozen rings pay 2 times over on pages two and three. It is fed only 3.3 times a run and tapped in 10% of rounds.
4. **Flood:** burner fills the water jar, and the tap re-closes rings. It pays when the rings stop. It is tapped in 24% of rounds and gives only 1.31 where tapped.
5. **Ammonia refire:** it fires the page twice, but it is unbuilt and has no molecules that carry NH2.

Plain bonuses and dead pieces:
- Carousel, dropper and timer are plain bonuses.
- The burner is dead alone (1.00) and harmful at random (0.72, lowering 19 of 31 rounds).
- The burette alone is 1.04 and the magnet 1.08. Reagents already do their work.
- Mirror at a random place is 0.36, so it punishes a novice who misplaces it.
- Wood spirit, ammonia and the passive timer and carousel all do "more reagents fire". Hydrogen, slime, acid, magnet and the bridge reagent all do "cross a gap".
- Seedling and the chain-on reagent both add a chunk.
- Salt (cures) has no combination I can find.

The docs also contradict each other. PIECES.md says the clamp was removed because wood spirit "protects one OH". HOMUNCULI.md says wood spirit fires all five reactions in random order. One is wrong.

Rules a player could not predict from the page:
- Mirror keeps the side the pointer is nearer to.
- A mirror must stay six under the size limit.
- Oxidize gives off carbon dioxide only at a chain-end C=C.
- A zip's last OH cannot be dehydrated.
- Frozen rings pay 2 times over.
- Water makes rings that "only close again" pay a quarter.

**Three most important fixes**

1. Rerun the decision gates on the final cascade rules and write down one pay rule. Also settle the wood spirit contradiction. Without that, section 2 rests on old numbers.
2. Give burner, burette and magnet a job alone, or cut them. As they stand they are halves of pairs. Merge the five gap-crossers into two or three distinct ones. Merge the creatures that all fire more reagents.
3. Build more live pieces and creatures before measuring section 3. Four built builds and one on paper is not enough, and the shelf and pair gates cannot be read on eight pieces.

## The numbers (section 1)

**Section 1 (the number to beat): fail. The numbers are not good enough, and they are stale.**

The newest full measure is the third one in RIG.md (committed 11:44 on 11-11). Since then these changes went in:
- Choosing the molecule (12:21), which removes the special kinds of round.
- Oxidize giving off carbon dioxide (`RULE.co2`).
- The homunculi table with water, hydrogen and dry ice.
- A changed player policy for using pieces. RIG.md says outright that the Par was fitted to players who wasted pieces.
- Uncommitted edits to `rules.js`, `path.js`, `scheme.js` and `tools_sim.js`.

The pass rates, margins and molecule spreads below predate all of that.

**Gates against the third measure**

1. **Measure size.** The gate asks for 500 or more runs per player. It has 205, 205, 268 and 120. Not met.
2. **Pass rate within 12 points of target.** Median player: 96, 97, 100, 98, 95, 87, 73, 64, 55 against 98, 95, 85, 92, 88, 70, 78, 70, 50. The worst miss is 17.8, in round 6 (87 against 70), so this is not met. The player is clearly above target in rounds 3 to 6, and the planner is far under its targets in rounds 7 to 9.
3. **Last page still undecided in 40% of rounds from round 4.** The last page changed the level reached in 68% of rounds. Met, but old.
4. **Median margin within 1.5 times of target.** Margins are 8.8, 22.6, 24.6, 12.1 and 6.0 in rounds 1 to 5, against targets of 3, 2.5, 1.6, 2 and 1.8. That is 3 to 15 times off, so it fails clearly. Rounds 7 to 9 are fine.
5. **Kinds of round within 2 times.** Kinds were 4.1 times apart before their factor and the measure did not refit them. Choosing the molecule then removed the special kinds, so the gate is unmeasured on the new rules.
6. **Molecules within 3 times in the same round.** This was 2.7 to 6.0 apart in the second measure and 17 apart before the factor in the third. It was not reported after the fit. Unmeasured.

The novice dies at round 4 (34% against 55%), and only 9 of 205 novices reach round 6. The planner is below its targets in the late rounds. The player policy changed after the measure, and the 40-run check on the new policy already shows different pass rates (100, 100, 100, 100, 90, 97, 86, 87, 73).

**Choosing the molecule.** It was checked on 200 median runs against the old Par. The number to beat still fits, with no round moving more than 5 points, but this is not the 500-run gate. The median player took the harder molecule in 14% of pairs, the redraw was never used, and the median player ends with 28 coins, so the choice has no weight. The harder molecule often has the lower number, which reads as the easier round.

**Can the creature numbers in HOMUNCULI.md be trusted?** No, not as shipped strengths.
- The table is from a small model, and the document says so. Most of its multipliers (1.5 to 2.3) have not been checked in the game.
- Water, hydrogen and dry ice were read in the game on 200 runs, and the gap shows. Hydrogen was predicted at 1.6 and read 1.29 at the median. Dry ice's feed changed from 3 to 1, and its freeze made 1.00 until the payout was raised to two times over. Dry ice was then tuned to sit in its band, which is fitting the number to the target.
- Each creature is lit in only 4 to 24% of rounds, because the simulated players do not feed on purpose. The all-rounds effect is 1.02 to 1.06, and the pass rate and the number to beat were not remeasured.
- The first run was at 1.5 steps in the 40-run sample, so the sample sizes are thin.

Before any creature ships:
- Measure each of the other seven in the game on 500 runs.
- Measure with players that feed the creature on purpose, and measure the creature's effect on pass rate.
- Make the NH2 and SH molecules, since ammonia and brimstone have only been tried on made-up ones.
- Refit Par with creatures held.
- Run salt, slime and seedling at their best taps.

**Three things to fix, most important first**
1. Run the full 500-run measure for every player, plus unseen seeds, on the current rules. Refit Par to the players as they now play, and record the planner and greedy rows.
2. Fix rounds 2 to 5. The median player is 6 to 25 times over the number, and the novice cliff at round 4 needs the gap between novice and median closed.
3. Measure kinds of round and molecule spread on the choose-two rules, and give the coins a sink so the harder molecule has a price.

## Progression and fun (sections 6 and 7)

**Sections 6 and 7: independent review (docs and code only, no play seen)**

**What is built.** The code in `scheme.js` and `path.js` has a saved credit count and a supplier page opened with credit. Stages 1 to 3 hide later layers. Round 3 beaten opens stage 2, and round 6 beaten twice opens stage 3. A first run teaches by showing one molecule. The molecule pair, the redraw and the coin purse appear in a later run. Three jars (water, hydrogen, dry ice) show a count like 38/200 and hatch a creature you can take into a run. Seven more creatures are stubs that cannot be offered.

**What is designed but not built.** `LOOP.md` itself lists these as not built, and the code agrees:
- The bench screen between runs.
- Kits.
- The book of real compounds.
- The record for each kit.
- Stakes and the win ladder.
- Helper deeds.
- The seals, the pact, the stone, seeds and the collection from `ARC.md`.
- Seven of the ten creatures.

All of this fails, because only built things can pass.

**Section 6, progression: fail.**
- **Run 1.** The player knows the goal: beat the number. Nothing is shown about what comes after it.
- **Run 5.** This is the worst gap. After a loss in stage 1, `next()` adds credit and starts a new run with no screen, so the credit is invisible. A lost run leaves credit and jar counts, but the player sees neither.
- **Jars.** Their counts show only from stage 2, and 200 water is a very slow bar. `HOMUNCULI.md` says the jars fill in about 6 to 8 runs, but a creature that is rarely lit (water 24% of rounds, hydrogen 4%) is hard to feel.
- **Suppliers.** Each costs credit and shows a price, which is a real counter. The family-opening cost is also shown.
- **Run 20 and run 60.** Nothing is built to work toward past the suppliers and three creatures. `LOOP.md` admits there is little left by run 60.
- **Unlocks.** Stage unlocks fire silently from deeds (beat round 3, beat round 6 twice). There is no object or count for them, so "every unlock is earned by play and seen as an object with a count" is not met. The "always know what I am working toward" test fails.
- **Coin sink.** The doc says the median player ends with 28 coins and nothing to spend them on. The molecule pair choice has little weight for the same reason.

**Section 7, fun: fail, with low confidence.** I saw no recordings, so this is from the numbers.
- **Early rounds.** The median player passes rounds 1 to 3 at 96 to 100%, with margins of 8.8, 22.6 and 24.6 against targets of about 2 to 3. This is the "tension dead when the number is beaten early" problem the earlier reviewers flagged, and `PASS.md` still lists it.
- **Sizes.** The 28 coins held and the 44.5% win rate for the median player point to a game that is easy first and hard late.
- **Another run.** Nothing visible pulls me back. A loss gives no screen, the credit is hidden, and the first unlock sits behind round 3.
- **What I would tell a friend.** The cascade is the one specific thing: a ring closes, the chain reels in, and the count grows. The progression gives me nothing else to say. The ten creatures in `HOMUNCULI.md` would give me something to say if they were built.

**Section 6: fail. Section 7: fail.**

**Top three fixes, most important first:**
1. Build the between-runs screen as objects. It should show the credit earned, the next supplier with its count, and the next stage unlock as a blank outline with its count. A lost run must always show something earned.
2. Give credit a sink in stage 1 and 2 that matters: coins and credit spent on something that changes the next run. Add an early reason to retry, such as the first kit and its outline with a count.
3. Make rounds 1 to 3 tense. Raise the early numbers so the median margin falls toward 2 to 3 times, and build creatures 4 to 10 so the 20-run and 60-run player has something to work toward.
