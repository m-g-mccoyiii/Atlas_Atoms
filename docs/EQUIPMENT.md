# Equipment: rarity, power and the plan to sixty

## Why a piece exists

Each piece changes how a round is best played. A piece that only adds a number is filler. Each belongs to one of three shelves, which are also its rarity:

| Shelf | Rarity | What it should be worth alone | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| lab | common and uncommon | 1.2 to 2.5 times a round | steady, no drawback |
| alchemy | rare | 2.5 to 4 times | strong, with a condition to meet |
| occult | very rare | 4 to 8 times | strongest, always with a price |

"Worth alone" is measured: the median round of a simulated player who keeps the row, on six chain molecules, with the piece and without it.

## First measurement, 2026-11-10

Before any balancing the spread ran from 0.3 to 1,850. Anything that multiplied on every step of a chain had become absurd now that chains run 20 steps.

Far too strong: reflux condenser 1,850, aqua regia 114, black candle 34, dropper 15, carousel 7.6, log book 7, goat 7, litmus paper 6.2 (a first-shelf piece), philosopher's stone 6, mortar 4.6, elixir 4.4, cold trap 4.2.

No measurable effect (inside the noise of the test): timer, clamp, recycler, homunculus, mirror, cannula, ouroboros, sieves, spatula, stir plate.

Changed: reflux row times 1.06 (was 1.7), aqua regia times 1.02 (was 1.06), black candle every 13th reaction times 3 (was the 13th times 13), dropper one more use (was two), carousel one more reagent (was two), log book +0.02 (was +0.04), litmus +0.08 (was +0.2), stone times 1.05 (was 1.12), goat times 1.07 (was 1.15).

## Second measurement, after the changes

The test itself is noisy: four runs with no equipment differed by a factor of 3.1, so only differences larger than about 3 mean anything. On that basis most pieces now sit between 1 and 5. Still clearly too strong: black candle 26 (cut again, to times 2), carousel 19, pact 9.6, dropper 8.4, elixir 7.8. The test needs many more plays a piece before finer tuning is worth doing.

Still to do: the ten with no measurable effect need rules that matter; every piece needs measuring again after each change.

## The goat

It is a goat sacrifice: a goat on a stone altar between two black candles, a knife laid in front of it. No blood. It takes one use of a reagent each page and multiplies extra on every reaction.

## To sixty

Forty exist. Twenty more, weighted toward the occult shelf and toward pieces that are funny. Each needs a rule that changes play, not a bigger number. Candidates, to be built one at a time, each drawn, blind-tested and measured:

Occult
1. Voodoo doll: the first place a reaction changes is changed twice.
2. Tarot card, the Tower: the first time a row breaks each round, it does not.
3. Monkey's paw: three wishes a run. A wish doubles a round's total and removes a reagent for good.
4. Cursed idol: every number is doubled, the number to beat as well. Cannot be put down.
5. Crystal ball: shows the next page's reagents now.
6. Black cat: a reaction that would make nothing makes 13.
7. Grimoire: the last reagent on each page is used twice.
8. Raven: carries one unused reaction over to the next page.
9. Hand of glory: equipment fires even on a broken row.
10. Hourglass of sand from a grave: one more page, and the number to beat grows by a tenth each page.

Funny
11. Rubber duck: says nothing. Extra +0.01 for every reaction it watches.
12. The intern: does one reaction of its own choosing each page. It is not always a good one.
13. Coffee: one more reaction a page for two pages, then one fewer for the rest of the round.
14. Safety goggles, on forehead: no effect. Sells back for double.
15. Grant proposal: pays out only if the round is beaten by ten times.
16. Unlabeled bottle: a different reagent every page.
17. Lab coat with someone else's name: copies the piece to its left.
18. Fire extinguisher: stops a chain where the player chooses and banks it at half again.
19. Motivational poster: row +0.5 on page one, nothing after.
20. Expired milk: grows. Extra +0.1 more each round it is kept.

## Terms set before a run

The player chooses how a run is weighted before it starts, the way Balatro has decks and stakes. A term changes odds or outcomes and is paid for by another term. Candidates:

- Starting shelf: begin with one piece from the lab, alchemy or occult shelf. A stronger start raises every number to beat.
- Odds: more rare offers and fewer common ones, or the reverse.
- Molecules: which families can be dealt.
- Pages: two long pages or four short ones.
- Stakes: numbers to beat times 1.5, pay doubled.
- Reagents: start with five reagents and one use each, or three and three uses.

None of these are built.
